# NuTak company interview research — first pass

Reviewed: 2026-10-16. Scope: major US technology employers and AI labs.
This is a source-backed research baseline, not a claim that all major companies or every role are covered. No application code or live AI integration is included.

## Evidence rules

- Separate company-published facts, NuTak practice design, and unverified reports.
- Company, role, seniority, region, and interview stage determine the practice. A company name alone is insufficient.
- Never describe generated questions as leaked, actual, guaranteed, or company-approved.
- Recruiter instructions and current role-specific candidate materials override general guidance.
- Show sources, scope, and last-reviewed date on each company profile.
- English and Russian interfaces use the same evidence. Russian practice does not imply an employer offers Russian interviews.
- Anya, Mila, and Vera are fictional AI interviewers, independent of the employers.
- Humor changes delivery, not assessment criteria. Do not mock accents, background, age, disability, or nervousness.
- Feedback must reference the user's actual answers, distinguish missing evidence from demonstrated weakness, and avoid hire/no-hire predictions.
- Published culture statements can inform practice themes but are not proof of an interview rubric.
- Recheck sources before launching a company track and after material hiring changes. No automatic monitoring has been configured.

## Company evidence and proposed practice

### Amazon — verified, role-specific
Official SDE II guide describes an online assessment followed by an interview loop; coding, system design, and behavioral evidence linked to Leadership Principles. It recommends concrete metrics when applicable and STAR structure. Do not generalize SDE II's round counts to every Amazon job.
NuTak design: ask about individual ownership, decisions, results, and lessons; add coding/design only for relevant roles.
Sources:
- https://amazon.jobs/content/en/how-we-hire/sde-ii-interview-prep
- https://amazon.jobs/content/en/how-we-hire/product-manager-interview-prep (discovered; detailed review pending)
- https://amazon.jobs/content/en/how-we-hire/applied-scientist-interview-prep (discovered; detailed review pending)

### Microsoft — verified general technical guidance
Microsoft describes role-relevant technical principles, problem solving, adaptability, strategic thinking, past experience, and role-related scenarios.
NuTak design: ask the candidate to clarify a problem, explain a solution, consider alternatives, and connect prior experience to the target role. These exercises are our design, not a published Microsoft scoring rubric.
Source: https://careers.microsoft.com/v2/global/en/hiring-tips/technical-interviewing.html

### Google — partial; current process detail needs further verification
The official hiring-process page was reachable but returned mostly navigation, not a complete process. A Google-published recruiter story from December 2021 describes practicing behavioral and hypothetical questions using STAR. This is historical preparation advice, not a current universal interview process.
NuTak design: offer general role-based practice; withhold exact Google loop claims until current candidate guidance is verified.
Sources:
- https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/how-we-hire/
- https://blog.google/company-news/inside-google/life-at-google/how-google-recruiter-prepared-her-own-interview/

### Google DeepMind — verified, separate from general Google
The careers page describes an introductory recruiter call, possible hiring-manager conversation, skills interviews, and final discussions involving leadership, team goals, mission and values. Exact steps vary by role.
NuTak design: research/engineering project discussion, evidence behind decisions, and explaining work to peers; tailor to the job.
Source: https://deepmind.google/careers/

### NVIDIA — verified general process
Official guidance describes discussions with hiring managers, team members and other groups; technical applicants may receive coding exercises. Interview formats vary by team. It explicitly prohibits unapproved outside tools during interviews.
NuTak design: role-specific technical explanation and problem solving. Practice takes place before the real interview; never imply permission to use NuTak during one.
Source: https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/about-nvidia/careers/how-we-hire/

### Apple — unverified process
Official job listings were found, but this pass did not verify a general corporate interview guide. An attempted how-we-hire page was inaccessible. Do not claim Apple uses a fixed company-wide sequence or invent evaluation criteria.
NuTak design: require a specific job description and label the session role-based practice until stronger company evidence is available.
Source for future role research: https://jobs.apple.com/en-us/search

### Netflix — verified culture, not a verified interview loop
The official culture memo emphasizes judgment, candor, responsibility, curiosity, and collaboration. This is cultural context, not evidence of exact interview rounds or scoring.
NuTak design: judgment under ambiguity, constructive disagreement, decisions and accountability. Label these as exercises inspired by published values.
Source: https://jobs.netflix.com/culture

### OpenAI — verified general interview guide
Official guidance describes introductory calls, team-dependent skills assessments, and final interviews. Assessment formats may include pair coding, take-home projects and technical tests. It values reasoning, communication and collaboration; engineering guidance mentions solution quality, performance and testing. AI-tool permission varies by interview.
NuTak design: explain decisions, solve relevant work samples, discuss testing and trade-offs, and examine what the candidate learned.
Source: https://openai.com/interview-guide/

### Anthropic — verified general careers guidance
Technical interviews use live coding tools such as Colab and CodeSignal. Guidance permits looking things up while emphasizing fluency with basics. Nontechnical conversations explore problem solving and motivation. Separate AI-use policy exists; permission to look up information must not be treated as permission to use AI.
NuTak design: practical technical reasoning where appropriate, motivation, and clear discussion of decisions. Inspect the linked AI-use policy before making specific tool-permission claims.
Source: https://www.anthropic.com/careers

### Adobe — verified role-dependent hiring guidance
The official process includes talent discussion, hiring-manager interview, team interviews and possible assessments. Technical examples include coding and system design; creative examples include briefs and case studies; other roles may use scenarios or presentations.
NuTak design: select the relevant assessment family rather than giving everyone coding questions.
Source: https://careers.adobe.com/us/en/hiring-process

### Salesforce — verified general guidance
Official guidance advises matching experience to the job description, using real situations, and preparing for behavioral, competency-based or situational questions, with knowledge of the business.
NuTak design: evidence of impact and role-relevant scenarios; customer-facing exercises only for suitable roles.
Source: https://www.salesforce.com/company/careers/culture/how-we-hire/

### Oracle — verified technical preparation, published September 2024
Official guidance covers data structures, algorithms, complexity, clarifying requirements, communicating a plan, coding and discussing tests. This is preparation guidance rather than a fixed current hiring loop.
NuTak design: coding practice with explanation, explicit assumptions and test cases.
Source: https://blogs.oracle.com/jobsatoracle/ace-your-technical-interview

### Tesla — verified internship-context preparation
The official intern resources link a two-page preparation guide. It emphasizes role fit, personal contributions, difficult challenges, resourcefulness, clear reasoning, assumptions and mission understanding. Scope is internship preparation; do not claim it defines all full-time interviews.
NuTak design: concrete project examples and constrained problem solving, adjusted to the target role.
Sources:
- https://www.tesla.com/careers/intern-resources
- https://digitalassets.tesla.com/tesla-contents/image/upload/preparing-for-your-interview_en

## Proposed product requirements derived from research

These are NuTak decisions, not claims made by employers.

1. Inputs: company, role, experience level, region, optional job description, interview stage, English/Russian, interviewer and humor level.
2. Separate interview difficulty from humor.
3. Offer a research card before starting: verified guidance, coverage limitations, sources, date.
4. Ask one question at a time; follow up on actual answers rather than reading a rigid script.
5. Keep a neutral evidence rubric underneath the three personalities.
6. Support behavioral practice first; clearly identify any technical capability not yet implemented, such as code execution.
7. Feedback: evidence shown, missing detail, one actionable improvement, retry. No hiring probability.
8. Every exercise is labeled simulated unless explicitly reproducing a short published example with attribution.
9. Keep privacy, provider retention and payment claims pending until the actual implementation is established.
10. Optional PayPal support must clearly support NuTak's costs, not imply payment to an employer or purchase of a hiring advantage.
